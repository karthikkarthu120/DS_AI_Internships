DATASET INFORMATION

Dataset Name: Customer Analytics Dataset

Description:
This dataset contains demographic and purchasing behavior
information of customers. Each row represents one customer.

Attributes:
- Age: Age of customer
- Gender: Gender category
- AnnualIncome: Yearly income
- SpendingScore: Customer spending tendency
- Education: Educational qualification
- City: Customer location
- PreferredDevice: Device used for purchases

Purpose:
The dataset is used for Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
to understand customer behavior patterns and relationships
between demographic and purchasing variables.
